🧮 Free-money scanner — markets that contradict themselves

not run yet A different kind of edge entirely. Everything else in this dashboard tries to forecast — and we proved that doesn't work here. This doesn't forecast anything. When a set of outcomes is mutually exclusive and exhaustive, exactly one of them pays $1, so their prices must sum to $1. If you can buy every outcome for less than a dollar, you are guaranteed a profit no matter what happens, and no skill or information is involved. The only questions are whether the gap survives fees and whether the books are deep enough to fill.
Scan:
EventOutcomesCost to own all Gross edgeFeesNet edgeMax size
Hit Scan for arbitrage. It reads every mutually-exclusive market set on Polymarket and checks whether the outcome prices add up.

Cost to own all = the sum of the best ask across every outcome — what you would actually pay right now, not the mid. Fees use Polymarket's real taker formula, 0.06 × shares × p × (1−p) per leg, which is brutal on baskets containing cheap legs. Max size is set by the thinnest leg in the basket, because a basket you can only half-fill is not an arbitrage — it is a directional bet you did not intend to make. A negative net edge means the market is behaving properly.

How to read "edge": a bet placed at 20¢ should win about 20% of the time if the market is efficient. Edge is how much more (or less) often those bets actually won. Positive edge on high-score bands means the detector is finding genuine information; an edge near zero means the score is decoration. Caveats: this tests trade-shape signals only (size, entry odds, market type, anonymity) — the live wallet-age, cluster and liquidity bonuses can't be reconstructed historically. Sampling is weighted toward higher-volume resolved markets. Small samples are noisy; treat anything under ~30 bets in a band as indicative only.

collecting The strategic bet: prediction markets are hard to trade but may be early. Every qualifying signal is timestamped with a snapshot of the instruments it should move, then re-checked at +4h, +24h and +72h. Over time this measures whether the detector actually leads real markets — the thing that would make all this information worth money.

Direction is derived mechanically: rising odds on a rate cut implies bonds and small caps up; rising odds on a military strike implies crude and gold up with equities down; rising tariff odds implies importers and semis down. 📉 Sports line movement — steam detector

needs a key The one signal that actually catches fixing: a line moving hard with no news, especially when every book moves together. This builds its own history by snapshotting odds over time — so the longer it runs, the more it sees.
Sport:

Add a free key from the-odds-api.com (500 requests/month, no card). Each snapshot costs one request; the tracker takes at most one every 30 minutes and only while this page is open.

Movement alone is not proof — lines move for injuries, weather, lineup news and ordinary sharp money. What investigators look for is a steam move: a large, fast, one-directional shift that most books make together, with no public explanation. This tracker can only see games it has snapshotted before, so a freshly-added sport shows nothing until the second snapshot.

🧰 Tracker arsenal — the best external detectors

How the suspicion score works (and its limits)
Size (0–45 pts): logarithmic in dollar amount — $10k ≈ 18 pts, $100k ≈ 36 pts, $1M+ caps at 45.
Longshot entry (0–28 pts): buying at ≤5¢ odds +28, ≤12¢ +22, ≤20¢ +14, ≤35¢ +7. Insiders buy cheap certainty — the Maduro bettor entered around 5–10¢; the Nobel bettor at 1–2¢.
News-sensitive market (+15): titles matching geopolitics, appointments, pardons, military action, regulatory decisions, launches — the categories where non-public information exists. Sports/esports game outcomes score −12 (still shown; match-fixing exists but game markets are mostly noise).
Anonymity (+4 to +8): auto-generated pseudonyms or bare wallets, no profile. 57% of wallets Polysights flagged were created within 24h of the suspicious trade.
Pre-resolution dump (+10): selling large size at ≥90¢ — locking in before an outcome others don't know is settled.
Liquidity impact (+12 to +20): the bet is compared to its market's 24h volume — a wager that is ≥10% (or ≥30%) of everything traded in that market all day is a conviction signal no deep-market trade can fake.
Wallet history (+8 to +15): for any trade scoring 55+, the wallet's full trade history is pulled automatically — a first-ever trade or a wallet under 7 days old gets +15 (57% of Polysights-flagged wallets were under 24h old). Click any trader for the full dossier: age, longshot ratio, P&L, biggest trade.
Repeat offenders (+10): wallets this page has flagged 3+ times before (memory persists in your browser) get boosted — serial suspicious timing is the strongest pattern of all.
Cluster detection (+12): 3+ distinct wallets taking the same side of the same market with $30k+ combined in one feed window triggers a cluster alert — the exact signature of the Feb 2026 Iran-strike wallets. News-sensitive clusters fire the sound/notification alert.
Repricing context (+8): buys into markets that have already moved ≥15pts in 24h — information is being priced in, and the odds-swings scanner shows you which markets are lurching before the headlines land.
Limits: it can't see funding paths or cross-wallet clusters (Bubblemaps and Polysights can — links in the arsenal). Kalshi traders are anonymous by design, so only trade-shape signals apply there. A high score is a reason to look closer, never proof. Curated case data is static as of Aug 2, 2026; the recurring monitor keeps you updated on new cases.
