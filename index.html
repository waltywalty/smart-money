🧰 Tracker arsenal — the best external detectors

How the suspicion score works (and its limits)
Size (0–45 pts): logarithmic in dollar amount — $10k ≈ 18 pts, $100k ≈ 36 pts, $1M+ caps at 45.
Longshot entry (0–28 pts): buying at ≤5¢ odds +28, ≤12¢ +22, ≤20¢ +14, ≤35¢ +7. Insiders buy cheap certainty — the Maduro bettor entered around 5–10¢; the Nobel bettor at 1–2¢.
News-sensitive market (+15): titles matching geopolitics, appointments, pardons, military action, regulatory decisions, launches — the categories where non-public information exists. Sports/esports game outcomes score −12 (still shown; match-fixing exists but game markets are mostly noise).
Anonymity (+4 to +8): auto-generated pseudonyms or bare wallets, no profile. 57% of wallets Polysights flagged were created within 24h of the suspicious trade.
Pre-resolution dump (+10): selling large size at ≥90¢ — locking in before an outcome others don't know is settled.
Liquidity impact (+12 to +20): the bet is compared to its market's 24h volume — a wager that is ≥10% (or ≥30%) of everything traded in that market all day is a conviction signal no deep-market trade can fake.
Wallet history (+8 to +15): for any trade scoring 55+, the wallet's full trade history is pulled automatically — a first-ever trade or a wallet under 7 days old gets +15 (57% of Polysights-flagged wallets were under 24h old). Click any trader for the full dossier: age, longshot ratio, P&L, biggest trade.
Repeat offenders (+10): wallets this page has flagged 3+ times before (memory persists in your browser) get boosted — serial suspicious timing is the strongest pattern of all.
Cluster detection (+12): 3+ distinct wallets taking the same side of the same market with $30k+ combined in one feed window triggers a cluster alert — the exact signature of the Feb 2026 Iran-strike wallets. News-sensitive clusters fire the sound/notification alert.
Repricing context (+8): buys into markets that have already moved ≥15pts in 24h — information is being priced in, and the odds-swings scanner shows you which markets are lurching before the headlines land.
Limits: it can't see funding paths or cross-wallet clusters (Bubblemaps and Polysights can — links in the arsenal). Kalshi traders are anonymous by design, so only trade-shape signals apply there. A high score is a reason to look closer, never proof. Curated case data is static as of Aug 2, 2026; the recurring monitor keeps you updated on new cases.
