### ASSUMED - with what would test it

**1. That H65's shape is a property of Kalshi at all.** It is confirmed **there**: on 201 events,

## 2b. A hard limit on the whole depth question - not a caveat, a ceiling

**Branch three cannot be completed. Not by waiting, not by collecting, not by paying.**

`archive.pmxt.dev` is **the only public source of Kalshi order-book depth that has ever existed**.
It is frozen at **`2026-06-11T03`** and has not resumed - re-verified 2026-08-18 with a positive
control beside the impossible one: known-present shard **206**, `2026-08-16T12` **404**,
`2026-08-17T12` **404**, impossible control **404**.

B1's branch three requires a **depth-source replication** before any positive result may be stated.
There is no second source to replicate against, and no amount of future collection creates one,
because the book is not retained by the exchange and the one party who archived it stopped.

So the correct phrase is **"unreportable with the data that exists"**, not "pending". The difference
matters: *pending* invites someone to come back to it, and nobody can.

**This is a ceiling on every future Kalshi depth question, not on one family.** Any depth study on
this venue is now bounded to `2026-05-14` -> `2026-06-11T03`, to the 41% of hours that pass
admission, and to quiet hours within those. It is recorded in `docs/INFRA.md` so it is findable
without reading a registry entry's footnotes.

**What would lift it:** a new public archive, a commercial book feed, or the project standing up its
own continuous depth collection from today forward - which buys the future, never the past.

## 6. What Phase B established is portable

A description of what transfers and what does not. It makes no recommendation and ranks nothing.

**The single most portable thing this run produced is one sentence, and it is not about Kalshi:**

> **A hurdle quoted without its family composition is not a hurdle**, in the same way that a price
> without size is not a price.

It earned that status by measurement. Two packets went into the size axis and the horizon axis; the
composition of the pool moved the number **2.05x more than both of them combined**. Nothing in that
result depends on the venue. Any pooled cost estimate - commissions and spread on an equity desk,
all-in financing cost across a book of borrows, blended take-rate across a payments mix - is
weighted by what is in the pool at least as much as by the variable it names. The failure mode is
that the pooled number looks like a measurement of the axis and is partly a measurement of the mix,
and it is invisible unless composition is reported beside the figure. It now sits in
`skills/research-method/SKILL.md` in venue-neutral form.

**What else transfers unchanged.** Pre-registration with the rule, the settling sample size, the
abandonment conditions and the meaning of every outcome, sealed and hashed before outcomes - it has
caught seven wrong premises here, and in H65 it was the sealed calling rule that stopped a
plausible-looking minimum from being reported. The unit-of-observation question asked first rather
than last. Obtainability as a gate separate from statistical validity. Cost measured rather than
assumed and expressed as a function of every axis it varies on. Controls that hold the measurement's
access level, plus a probe that must succeed alongside the one that must fail. Counterbalanced arms
with the source held constant. "Could not establish" kept distinct from a null. Right-for-the-wrong-
reason as a standing check on premises. The instrument-failure catalogue in the synthesis is
venue-neutral except for the endpoint names.

**What does not transfer.** The fifteen mechanisms are prediction-market specific in their
particulars - several have obvious analogues elsewhere, and every one of those would need
re-measuring rather than re-assuming. The per-endpoint filter-honouring table, the schema, the
live/historical boundary and the fee model are Kalshi facts and nothing else. The dataset itself is
five years of one venue.

**What a different venue would need built.** A different data layer: Kalshi's public API needs no
credentials and no vendor, which is unusual. A different hurdle magnitude - the cost of crossing
here is measured in **hundreds of basis points**, and an equity commission-plus-spread hurdle is
measured in **single digits**; that is a hundredfold change in what counts as an edge, and it
changes the required sample size and the precision of every measurement, though not the method. And
a different obtainability question: H61 died on sub-60-second detect-to-fill against a five-minute
cron, and whether a filing-based or fundamentals-based signal has a friendlier latency structure is
exactly the sort of thing this method would insist on measuring rather than assuming.

**One structural lesson that does transfer, from section 2b.** The depth ceiling is a Kalshi fact in
its particulars and a general one in its shape: a research programme can come to rest on a single
external archive, and when that archive stops, a class of question closes permanently rather than
temporarily. Nothing about the method prevents that. What the method does is make it **visible and
named** at the moment it happens, instead of surfacing years later as a series of studies that were
quietly never finished.
