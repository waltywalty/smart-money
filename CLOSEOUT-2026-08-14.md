## 1. What is now known about retention, and what is still assumed

**Known.** Kalshi splits data into a **live** set and a **historical** set at a boundary you can
query: `GET /historical/cutoff` returned `market_settled_ts: 2026-06-14T00:00:00Z`. Markets
settling before it are served by a parallel endpoint family — `/historical/markets`,
`/historical/markets/{ticker}/candlesticks`, `/historical/trades`, `/historical/fills`,
`/historical/orders`, `/historical/positions`. Verified: `/historical/markets?event_ticker=HIGHNY-21AUG06`
returns a market from **2021** with its settlement result, while `/markets` returns zero rows for
it under every status filter. Candlesticks likewise — the historical path returns 9 candles for
`KXHIGHNY-26JUN03-T83` where the live path 404s. The **live** set's floor is real, uniform and
time-based: 2026-06-08 on 2026-08-14, identical across sixteen series carrying between 25 and
6,348 events, and one day later than the day before. That uniformity across three orders of
magnitude of volume excludes count-based expiry. `KXRAIN`'s apparent anomaly is series age — 24
events in total, cursor exhausted, earliest `KXRAIN-26AUG01`.

**Assumed.** That the live floor keeps advancing a day per day — two timepoints give a direction,
not a law. That `/historical` has no depth limit of its own; 2021 is the oldest thing tested, not
a demonstrated floor. That unauthenticated access reaches as far as authenticated (untested — it
needs credentials this project does not hold). The ten-day census converts the first into a
measurement.

## 5. What could not be completed, and why

**T2's data file.** There is no file channel between the Kernel VM, which can reach Kalshi, and
the Cowork sandbox, which holds the repo. Kalshi is 403 from Cowork; GitHub's API is reachable
from Kernel but the stored PAT now returns 401. Moving 13,832 rows through the conversation was
attempted, encoded to 87 KB, and abandoned — re-emitting base64 by hand is not reliable and it
corrupted on the first chunk. The counts, the cross-check and the per-series concentration were
all computed and are recorded above; the row-level file was not written.

**This is worth fixing once rather than working around repeatedly.** A fresh GitHub PAT with
contents:write would let the Kernel VM commit directly, and the whole patch-and-apply cycle would
disappear with it.
